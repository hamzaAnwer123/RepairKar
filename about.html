About RepairKar

Repair help, closer to home.

RepairKar helps people find verified mechanics, discuss a repair before booking, and keep the full service conversation in one place.

Find a mechanicContact support

Why it exists

A clearer way to get repairs done

Finding the right repair professional can be difficult when service details, location, timing, and communication are scattered. RepairKar brings those steps together so customers and mechanics can work from the same conversation.

Discover locally

Browse mechanics by service category, location, availability, and profile details.

Discuss first

Ask questions and share photos, files, voice notes, or location details before work begins.

Book with context

Create a booking with the service, location, schedule, and conversation already connected.

Stay informed

Track booking status, unread messages, mechanic activity, and completed work.
