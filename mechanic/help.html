Frequently asked questions

How do I receive booking requests?

Turn your availability switch on from the dashboard. Keep the page open while working so RepairKar can refresh nearby pending requests. Review the address and service before accepting.

What happens after I accept a request?

The booking moves to Accepted. Use chat or the call controls to coordinate with the customer, then mark it En Route when you are travelling and Completed after the repair is finished.

How do I manage my services?

Open My Gigs to add, edit, or deactivate a service. Use an accurate title, description, price range, and category so customers can choose the right repair.

Can I share files or location in chat?

Yes. Open the conversation connected to the booking to exchange messages, photos, documents, voice notes, and location details. Keep sensitive account information out of chat.

How do I update my mechanic profile?

Open Profile to update your workshop name, category, bio, photo, contact details, and location. Accurate information helps customers find and contact you.
